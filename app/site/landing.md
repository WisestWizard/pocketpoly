# POCKET POLY

POCKET POLY is a stereo hybrid polysynth for the M-VAVE FM-1, in development: eight voices of analog shapes, phase
distortion or wavetables with sync, ring and cross modulation, a resonant filter with drive, three envelopes, two
LFOs, an eight-slot modulation matrix and four macros, through an ensemble, a tape delay and a plate reverb; an
arpeggiator with Euclidean rhythm, chance, ratchets and a Turing machine; DICE, MUTATE and MORPH for patches. Eight
factory patches, sixteen user slots, velocity and pressure from the keys, MIDI over USB, Bluetooth (to a computer
or phone, and to a Bluetooth MIDI keyboard or controller) and TRS, USB audio out, and a screen reader.

Version {{VERSION}}. Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.
